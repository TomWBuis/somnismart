Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information somnismart.com handles and how.

Your questionnaire answers

The mattress questionnaire (such as sleep position, height, weight, body measurements, health conditions, and preferences) is processed entirely in your web browser. Your answers are not sent to SomniSmart or stored on any server, and they are cleared when you leave or reload the page. We do not have accounts or a database of your answers.

Third-party services that load with the page

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads styling and fonts from third-party providers (Tailwind CSS CDN and Google Fonts). Like any web server, these providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested. SomniSmart does not run its own analytics or advertising trackers.

Affiliate links and cookies

Some Shop links are affiliate links. When you click one, you leave somnismart.com, and the affiliate network (such as Impact, Awin, CJ, Rakuten, or Amazon) or the brand may set cookies or use similar technology to record that you came from SomniSmart, so a purchase can be credited. Those cookies are set and governed by the third party under its own privacy policy, not by SomniSmart. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

We do not sell your data

SomniSmart does not sell, rent, or share personal information. Because questionnaire answers never leave your browser, we have none to sell.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
