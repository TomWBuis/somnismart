Frequently asked questions

What mattress firmness is best for most people?

+

For most adults, a medium-firm mattress (around 6 on a 10-point scale) is the best starting point. A systematic review of 24 controlled trials found medium-firm mattresses best supported sleep comfort, sleep quality, and spinal alignment (Radwan et al., Sleep Health, 2015). From there, SomniSmart adjusts for your sleep position, body weight, and body shape.

What mattress is best for back pain?

+

Evidence favors medium-firm over very firm. In a randomized, double-blind trial of 313 adults with chronic low-back pain, people on medium-firm mattresses had less pain in bed, less pain on rising, and less disability after 90 days than people on firm mattresses (Kovacs et al., The Lancet, 2003). A mattress should keep your spine in a neutral line; if pain persists, see a healthcare professional.

What is the best mattress for side sleepers?

+

Side sleepers usually do best on a slightly softer, medium feel with a pressure-relieving comfort layer, so the shoulders and hips can sink in while the waist stays supported. Broad-shouldered side sleepers often need extra shoulder relief, so SomniSmart uses dress shirt neck size as a guide for that.

What firmness should back and stomach sleepers choose?

+

Back sleepers generally do well on medium-firm, which supports the lower back without flattening its natural curve. Stomach sleepers usually need firmer support so the hips don't sink and arch the lower back. Combination sleepers tend to suit medium to medium-firm, with a responsive surface that makes turning over easy.

What mattress is best for heavier or lighter people?

+

Body weight changes how firm a mattress feels. People over about 230 lb usually need firmer, thicker mattresses with higher-density foams or coils so they don't bottom out, while people under about 130 lb often find a softer feel more comfortable because they sink in less. SomniSmart adjusts its picks in both directions.

Why does SomniSmart ask for height, waist, and hip size?

+

Weight alone doesn't describe how you load a mattress. Height lets us estimate BMI (weight relative to height), and waist and hip measurements show body shape. People with wider hips and a narrower waist can feel their pelvis tilt on a mattress that is too firm, so for that shape we cap firmness at medium-firm and favor zoned support or a softer comfort layer.

What mattress is best for couples with different preferences?

+

Look for good motion isolation, such as foam or pocketed coils, so one partner's movement doesn't wake the other. If your firmness needs are very different, a split king (two twin XL mattresses on one frame) lets each person have their own feel. SomniSmart lets you enter both sleepers so the picks consider each of you.

What mattress is best for hot sleepers?

+

Hot sleepers usually do better with breathable designs, such as hybrids with coils that let air move, latex, or foams designed to shed heat, along with breathable covers. Dense, all-foam mattresses that you sink deeply into tend to trap more heat.

Foam, hybrid, innerspring, or latex: what's the difference?

+

All-foam mattresses contour closely and isolate motion well, but some sleep warm. Hybrids pair coils with foam or latex comfort layers for support, bounce, and airflow. Innerspring mattresses are bouncy and breathable but usually offer less pressure relief, while latex is responsive, durable, and naturally sleeps cooler than most foams.

What's the difference between organic, natural, and certified-safe materials?

+

Organic means the materials carry independent organic certification, such as GOTS for organic cotton and wool or GOLS for organic latex. Natural means plant or animal materials like latex, wool, or cotton, which are not necessarily certified organic. Commonly accepted as safe usually means foams certified by CertiPUR-US and products certified GREENGUARD Gold for low chemical emissions.

How long does a mattress last, and when should I replace it?

+

Lifespan depends on materials, build quality, and body weight, so there is no single number. Replace a mattress when it has visible sagging or body impressions, when you wake up stiff or sore and feel better sleeping elsewhere, or when you sleep noticeably worse than you used to.

Is there a break-in period, and should I use a sleep trial?

+

Yes. A new mattress often feels firmer at first and takes a few weeks to soften while your body adjusts, so give it some time before deciding. Buy from a brand with a home sleep trial and a clear return policy, and read the terms before you order.
